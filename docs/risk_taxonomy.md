# AI Risk Taxonomy (v0.1)

## 1. Risk Categories

### 1.1 Regulatory Risk
- Non-compliance with AI Act
- Data protection violations (GDPR)
- Financial regulatory breaches

### 1.2 Ethical Risk
- Bias and discrimination
- Lack of transparency
- Lack of explainability

### 1.3 Operational Risk
- Model drift
- Monitoring failure
- Incident response gaps

### 1.4 Security Risk
- Model extraction attacks
- Data leakage
- Prompt injection (LLM systems)

### 1.5 Strategic Risk
- Vendor lock-in
- Over-reliance on automation
- Reputational impact

---

## 2. Risk Dimensions

Each risk is evaluated along:

- Severity (Low / Medium / High)
- Likelihood (Low / Medium / High)
- Detectability (Low / Medium / High)

Composite Risk Score = f(Severity, Likelihood, Detectability)

---

## 3. Governance Controls Mapping

Each risk must map to:

- Preventive controls
- Detective controls
- Corrective controls
